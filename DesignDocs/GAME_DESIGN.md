# Game Design

## Concept

A military-future isometric tower defense game where the player builds a tactical kill zone against mechanized enemy waves.

## Core Loop

1. Preview the incoming wave.
2. Build or upgrade towers.
3. Keep at least one enemy path open.
4. Survive the wave.
5. Earn credits.
6. Adapt the maze, towers, and commander abilities.

## First Mission

- One isometric battlefield
- 5-8 waves
- Five tower types
- Six enemy types
- Boss wave
- Victory and defeat states

## Towers

- Rifle Turret: cheap baseline damage.
- Missile Battery: splash damage.
- Railgun: high damage, slow fire rate.
- EMP Spire: support tower that slows enemies.
- Drone Nest: flexible autonomous drones.

## Enemies

- Scout: fast and fragile.
- Armored Carrier: slow and durable.
- Shield Drone: protects nearby units.
- Swarm Bot: weak but numerous.
- Jammer: reduces nearby tower fire rate.
- Siege Walker: boss enemy.

## Commander Abilities

- Airstrike: area burst damage.
- EMP Pulse: area slow and shield break.
- Emergency Repair: restores base integrity.
